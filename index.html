Down payment sources (couple)

HBP: up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs (since Apr 16 2024); repay 1/15 per year. Temporary relief: first withdrawals 2022–2028 start repaying in the 5th year after withdrawal (e.g. 2026 → 2031); otherwise the 2nd year. FHSA: $8,000/yr, $40,000 lifetime contributions per person, withdrawals incl. growth tax-free for a qualifying first home; can be combined with HBP. Funds must be in place (and RRSP contributions held 90+ days) before closing — confirm with your bank.
